Oracle Payables (AP) Interview Questions

The questions interviewers ask on Oracle Fusion Payables, Payments and Expenses projects, with answers you can say in the room. Tap a question to see the answer.

40 questions 17 basic 23 in-depth
1What are the key Payables setups in Fusion?SetupBasic
  • Payables and Procurement options (accounting options, prepayment, conversion rates).
  • Invoice options, payment options, tax and withholding.
  • Invoice tolerances and hold/release names.
  • Payment terms, distribution sets.
  • Invoice approval (BPM rules).
  • Payment methods, payment process profiles, disbursement bank accounts.
  • Supplier setups with sites and assignments.
2What are distribution sets and when do you use them?InvoicesBasic
  • Templates that automatically create invoice distributions — full (fixed amounts/percentages) or skeleton (accounts only).
  • Example: monthly utility invoice split 40/60 between two cost centers.
  • Assigned to supplier sites as defaults.
3How do you match an invoice to a PO, a receipt and a consumption advice?InvoicesIn-depth
  • PO match: match to PO schedules (quantity × price or amount).
  • Receipt match: match to specific receipts (3-way), useful for price variance by receipt.
  • Consumption advice match: for consignment inventory — matching to consumption.
  • Matching option on the PO schedule determines whether AP must match to receipts or POs.
4What are quick credits and price corrections?InvoicesIn-depth
  • Price correction: adjusts the unit price of a previously matched invoice without changing quantity (e.g. supplier increases price after original invoice).
  • Quick credit: a credit memo that fully reverses an existing invoice (all lines) in one step.
  • Quantity correction via credit memo matched to the original.
5How do you set up hold resolution routing?HoldsIn-depth
  • Holds can route to owners via holds resolution workflow (e.g. price holds to buyer, quantity received holds to requester).
  • Rules by hold type and attributes; notifications with actions (release, request info).
  • Measure days on hold by type and owner.
6Walk me through the components of the Fusion Payments setup.PaymentsIn-depth
  • Payment methods (check, EFT, wire) with usage rules (by BU, currency, supplier).
  • Payment formats (BI Publisher templates).
  • Payment process profiles: format, transmission, grouping, limits, document numbering.
  • Payment system and transmission configurations (SFTP, web service).
  • Disbursement bank accounts and payment documents (check stock).
  • Funds disbursement process run via PPRs.
7How do you set up payment approval for large payment runs?PaymentsIn-depth
  • Enable payment approval in payment options; define approval rules (BPM) by amount, currency, bank account.
  • PPR stops for approval after build; approvers review and approve/reject payments.
  • Also consider "stop process for review" settings for manual review of proposed payments.
8How do you handle supplier refunds and outbound payments to non-suppliers?PaymentsIn-depth
  • Supplier refunds (supplier pays you): record as a negative payment (refund) against a credit memo, or receipt in AR if appropriate.
  • Payment requests: pay employees, customers (refunds) or one-time payees without creating suppliers — from AR refunds, Expenses or manual requests.
9How do corporate card expenses get from the card issuer to payment?ExpensesIn-depth
  • Card issuer transaction file loaded daily → transactions matched to employees (card numbers).
  • Employees add details/receipts and submit in expense reports.
  • Approval and audit.
  • Payment: company pays the card issuer (company pay) via AP invoice to the issuer; individual pay reimbursed to employees.
10How do expense audits work in Fusion?ExpensesIn-depth
  • Audit rules select reports (by amount, policy violations, random sampling, specific employees/expense types).
  • Auditors review receipts, adjust/reject lines, return to employees.
  • Receipt management: track missing/received receipts; imaged receipts accepted.
  • Audit list rules for repeat offenders.
11What is "sweep" in Payables close and when do you use it?Period CloseBasic
  • At period close, unaccounted transactions (invoices/payments that can't be accounted in the period) can be swept to the next open period so the period can close.
  • Use only after resolving what you can; review the swept list with accounting.
12What is your approach to AP automation for a contract role?InterviewBasic
  • Assess current state: invoice channels, touchless rate, holds by type, cycle time, cost per invoice.
  • Quick wins: tolerances, PO-level matching for BPAs, auto-approval of matched invoices, supplier data cleanup.
  • Medium-term: IDR, supplier portal/e-invoicing, AI agents for hold triage and supplier inquiries.
  • Measure monthly and report to the controller.
13How would you architect an AP automation program end to end?Accounts PayableIn-depth
  • Inbound channels: e-invoicing (EDI, PEPPOL, supplier portal), IDR for PDFs, paper scanning only as last resort.
  • Validation: PO matching, tolerances, duplicate detection, tax validation.
  • Approval: rules-based with auto-approval for matched invoices.
  • AI agents for exception handling (e.g. suggest resolution for holds).
  • Payments: optimized timing (discounts vs cash), virtual card programs.
  • Analytics: touchless rate, cycle time, cost per invoice.
  • Supplier enablement program as part of the architecture.
14Explain the Procure-to-Pay flow and where AP fits in.Accounts PayableBasic
  • Requisition → Purchase Order (Procurement)
  • Receipt of goods/services (Receiving)
  • Invoice entered/imported, matched to PO and receipt (AP)
  • Validation — matching, tax and holds checked (AP)
  • Approval (AP)
  • Payment through a Payment Process Request (AP / Payments)
  • Accounting — Create Accounting and transfer to GL (SLA)

Accounting entries: receipt accrual (Dr Inventory/Expense, Cr Receipt Accrual), invoice (Dr Accrual, Cr Liability), payment (Dr Liability, Cr Cash Clearing), reconciliation (Dr Cash Clearing, Cr Cash).

15What is the difference between 2-way, 3-way and 4-way matching?Accounts PayableBasic
  • 2-way: invoice vs PO (quantity and price). Used for services or low-risk items.
  • 3-way: invoice vs PO vs receipt — you can only be invoiced for what you received.
  • 4-way: invoice vs PO vs receipt vs inspection/acceptance — used where quality checks matter.

The match level is set on the PO schedule (defaulted from the supplier, item or purchasing options). Mismatches beyond tolerances put the invoice on hold.

16What are invoice holds and how do you release them?Accounts PayableBasic

Holds stop an invoice from being paid until an issue is fixed.

Common holds

  • Matching holds: Qty Ord, Qty Rec, Price, Amount.
  • Variance holds: distribution/tax variance.
  • Account holds: invalid distribution account.
  • Supplier/site holds: payment hold on the supplier.
  • Manual holds placed by users.

Release

  • System holds release automatically when you fix the cause and re-validate (e.g. receive the goods, correct the price).
  • Manual-releasable holds are released by an authorized user with a release reason.
  • Holds can route to the right person via the holds resolution workflow.
17What types of invoices can you enter in Payables?Accounts PayableBasic
  • Standard — normal supplier invoice.
  • Credit memo — supplier credit (negative).
  • Debit memo — your own credit against a supplier.
  • Prepayment — advance payment, applied later to invoices.
  • Withholding tax — created automatically for withheld amounts.
  • Expense report — employee reimbursement (from Expenses).
  • Payment request — non-supplier payees (e.g. refunds).
  • Interest — late-payment interest (auto-created when set up).
18How does a prepayment work in AP?Accounts PayableBasic
  • Enter a Prepayment invoice, validate and pay it. Accounting: Dr Prepaid Expense, Cr Liability, then Cr Cash on payment.
  • When the real invoice arrives, apply the prepayment to it — this reduces the amount payable. Accounting reverses the prepaid asset into the invoice.
  • Temporary prepayments can be applied to invoices; permanent ones (e.g. deposits) cannot.
  • Prepayments can also be applied automatically at validation if set up.

Tip: Mention reconciling the prepaid account — unapplied prepayments are a common audit question.

19What is a Payment Process Request (PPR)?Accounts PayableBasic

A PPR is how Fusion selects and pays invoices in bulk.

  • Selection criteria: pay-through date, business unit, supplier, payment method, pay group, currency.
  • Payment and processing options: which disbursement bank account, payment process profile, whether to stop for review.
  • Steps: select invoices → review proposed payments → build payments → create payment file (or print checks) → transmit to the bank.
  • Can be scheduled (e.g. weekly check run, daily ACH).

Payment Process Profiles define the format (NACHA, ISO 20022 XML, check) and transmission method.

20How do you handle 1099 reporting in Oracle Payables?Accounts PayableIn-depth
  • Mark US suppliers as federal reportable, with taxpayer ID and income tax type (e.g. MISC, NEC).
  • Invoice distributions carry the income tax type — defaulted from the supplier.
  • During the year, fix errors with the Update and Report Income Tax Details process.
  • At year end, run the 1099 reports (US 1099 Report, Invoice Exceptions, Supplier Exceptions) and produce the electronic file (1099 Electronic Media) or forms.

Tip: Mention reviewing the exception reports in December, not January.

21Explain the Oracle Expenses flow.ExpensesBasic
  • Employee creates an expense report (mobile app or web), attaches receipts; corporate card transactions can import automatically.
  • Expense policies check limits (e.g. daily meal limit) and required receipts.
  • Approval by the manager (and others via rules).
  • Auditor review (sample or rule-based), receipt verification.
  • Approved reports become Payables invoices (Process Expense Reimbursements) and are paid via a PPR or payroll.
  • Accounting goes to the expense accounts defaulted from the employee's cost center and expense type.
22What is the difference between a supplier, a supplier site and a site assignment?Accounts PayableBasic
  • Supplier — the company you buy from (name, tax ID, type).
  • Supplier address — physical address.
  • Supplier site — the address used for purchasing and/or paying, created per procurement business unit, with payment terms, payment method, pay group and holds.
  • Site assignment — lets a client business unit use that site (e.g. a shared-services BU processes invoices for many BUs).

Site assignments are a common reason a user "can't find the supplier" in a BU.

23How do payment terms and discounts work in AP?Accounts PayableBasic
  • Payment terms define due date(s) and early-payment discounts — e.g. 2/10 Net 30: 2% discount if paid within 10 days, full amount due in 30.
  • Terms default from the supplier site (or PO) onto the invoice; the terms date basis (invoice date, goods received, system date) decides the starting date.
  • Installments are created from the terms; the PPR picks them up when due.
  • Discounts are taken automatically if the payment date falls within the discount period and the option is enabled.
24What is the difference between invoice validation and invoice approval?Accounts PayableBasic
  • Validation is a system check: matching to PO/receipt, tolerances, tax, accounting, distribution totals — it places or releases holds.
  • Approval is a business decision by people (managers, cost center owners) routed by approval rules.
  • An invoice usually needs both before it can be paid; the order (validate first or approve first) is configurable.
25How do you cancel an invoice, and what happens to the accounting?Accounts PayableBasic
  • You can cancel only unpaid invoices (void the payment first if paid) with no applied prepayments.
  • Cancel from the Invoices work area; Oracle reverses the distributions and creates cancellation accounting (reversing the liability and expense) on the cancel date.
  • If the invoice was matched to a PO, the PO quantity billed is reduced.
  • Cancelled invoices remain for audit; you can't undo a cancellation.
26A supplier is paid twice. How would you investigate and prevent it from happening again?Accounts PayableIn-depth

Investigate

  • Find both invoices: check invoice numbers, amounts, dates and supplier sites — duplicates often differ by a character, a leading zero or a different site.
  • Check the source: one manual and one from OCR/imaging or an integration is the most common pattern.
  • Check whether the duplicate-invoice validation was bypassed or the invoice number was altered.

Recover

  • Contact the supplier for a refund or credit memo; record it in AP and apply it, or net it on the next payment.
  • Make sure accounting and 1099/tax reporting stay correct.

Prevent

  • Tighten duplicate-invoice checks (supplier + number + amount + date) and normalize invoice numbers on import.
  • Use holds and validation consistently; control who can override.
  • Run a periodic duplicate-payment analytics report before each payment run.
  • Review the imaging/integration process so the same invoice can't enter by two routes.

Tip: Interviewers listen for root cause + control, not just "we got the money back".

27How would you increase touchless invoice processing?Accounts PayableIn-depth
  • Push suppliers to PO-based invoicing with accurate POs (catalogs, agreements).
  • Use IDR for capture and supplier portal / e-invoicing (EDI, PEPPOL) to remove paper.
  • Tune matching tolerances and use receipt-based matching where appropriate.
  • Fix master data (supplier sites, bank details, UOMs).
  • Auto-approval for PO-matched invoices within tolerance.
  • Analyze holds by type and supplier monthly; fix root causes.
  • KPI: touchless %, invoices per FTE, average cycle time.
28How do you handle invoice matching for services or blanket POs where receipts are messy?Accounts PayableIn-depth
  • For services, consider 2-way match or amount-based lines instead of quantity.
  • For blanket POs with many receipts, consider moving the invoice match option from Receipt to Purchase Order (still 3-way) so AP doesn't have to pick specific receipts — Oracle checks received quantity in total.
  • Use receipt acceptance or self-billing (pay on receipt) for high-volume suppliers.
  • Train requesters to receive promptly.

Trade-off: PO-level matching loses receipt-level traceability for price differences; agree with audit.

29How would you roll out Oracle Expenses to 5,000 employees?ExpensesIn-depth
  • Policies harmonized across countries; travel policy owners sign off.
  • Corporate card feeds tested with the issuer; card holder matching.
  • Mobile app with receipt capture; single sign-on.
  • Approval via manager hierarchy (needs accurate HCM data).
  • Pilot with one department; fix issues; then waves.
  • Training: short videos and FAQs; help desk ready.
  • Auditing: risk-based audit rules to reduce manual audit.
  • Integration with payroll for reimbursements if used.
30How would you design approvals for non-PO invoices across 15 business units?Accounts PayableIn-depth
  • Base routing on cost center manager hierarchy (from HCM) with amount tiers.
  • BU-specific approval groups only where needed.
  • Auto-approval for small amounts/trusted suppliers with post-review.
  • Fallback approver when hierarchy data is missing.
  • Monitor approval cycle time and rejection rates; fix HCM data issues fast.
31How do you configure invoice approval workflow in Fusion Payables?Accounts PayableIn-depth
  • Enable invoice approval in Manage Invoice Options.
  • Define rules in BPM (Manage Invoice Approval Rules), e.g.:
  • PO-matched invoices within tolerance → no approval;
  • non-PO invoices → cost center manager by amount;
  • over $100k → add controller.
  • Use approval groups or supervisor hierarchy; handle missing approvers with a fallback.
  • Decide whether approval happens before or after validation and whether payment requires approval.
  • Test reassign, delegate, request information and rejection flows.
32What is Intelligent Document Recognition (IDR) / invoice imaging?Accounts PayableIn-depth
  • Suppliers email invoices (PDF/images) to a dedicated address.
  • Oracle's IDR (AI-based capture) reads the invoice and creates an incomplete invoice with header and line data, matched to PO when possible.
  • AP staff review and complete exceptions in the Invoices work area.
  • Higher accuracy over time as it learns supplier layouts.

What I'd watch

  • Supplier communication and email rules.
  • Duplicate detection (invoice sent by email and mail).
  • KPIs: touchless rate, processing time.
33How do you set up a new payment format, for example a bank's ACH file?PaymentsIn-depth
  • Get the bank's file specification and test process.
  • Use or copy a seeded BI Publisher template (e.g. NACHA, ISO 20022 pain.001) and adjust for the bank's requirements.
  • Create the payment format pointing to the template.
  • Create a payment process profile linking format, payment method, transmission (SFTP/ web service) and grouping rules.
  • Assign the profile to the disbursement bank account and payment methods.
  • Test with the bank: prenotes, small live payments, acknowledgment files.
34What is positive pay?PaymentsBasic

A fraud-prevention service: you send the bank a file of issued checks (number, amount, payee). The bank only pays checks that match.

  • In Fusion, run the Positive Pay File process (with a BIP template in the bank's format) after each check run.
  • Includes voided checks.
  • Transmit automatically via SFTP.
  • Exceptions are reviewed by treasury before the bank pays.
35How do you void a payment and what happens to the invoices and accounting?PaymentsIn-depth
  • Void the payment in Payables with a void date and action for the invoices: cancel, place on hold, or leave open for repayment.
  • Accounting reverses the payment entry (Dr Cash Clearing, Cr Liability) on the void date.
  • If the payment cleared the bank, you can't simply void — handle via stop payment or refund instead.
  • For electronic payments, void only if the bank rejected the payment.
  • Watch 1099 impact and positive pay files (send voids).
36How do you configure expense policies and corporate cards?ExpensesIn-depth
  • Expense templates and types (meals, hotel, mileage) with default accounts.
  • Policies — rate limits (by location, job level), daily limits, receipt requirements, justification thresholds; policy warnings vs errors.
  • Mileage rates by period/vehicle.
  • Corporate cards: card program, card issuer file loading (e.g. Amex, Visa feeds), matching to employees, payment options (company pay vs individual pay).
  • Audit rules and receipt management.
37How do you prevent supplier bank account fraud in Oracle?Accounts PayableIn-depth
  • Restrict who can create or change supplier bank accounts (separate from AP processing).
  • Require bank account verification — supplier portal changes go through approval (supplier profile change requests).
  • Callback procedures to a known contact before approving changes.
  • Audit reports on bank account changes before each payment run.
  • Hold first payments to a new account for review.

Tip: Mention a real control and the SoD conflicts you checked.

38What are the key Payables options and invoice options you configure?Accounts PayableIn-depth
  • Invoice options: invoice approval, allow adjustments to paid invoices, duplicate invoice checking, invoice currency, receipt acceptance days, default payment terms basis, require validation before approval.
  • Matching: tolerances, allow distribution override, recalculation on match.
  • Payment options: allow payment date before system date, bank charge bearer, payment document options.
  • Accounting option: accrual or cash basis, automatic offsets, prepayment account.
  • Self-service invoices and supplier portal options.

Each is set by business unit.

39How do you set up and use payment terms with installments?Accounts PayableIn-depth
  • Define the payment terms with multiple lines: e.g. 50% due in 30 days, 50% due in 60 days, with optional discounts per line.
  • Invoices using the terms get installments created automatically, each with its own due date and amount.
  • The payment process picks up each installment when due.
  • Useful for large purchases and contracts with staged payments.
40How do you handle an employee who submitted an expense report with a policy violation?ExpensesBasic
  • Policy rules flag the line (warning or error).
  • Warnings may require a justification from the employee; errors block submission.
  • The manager sees violations during approval; auditors can reject or adjust lines.
  • Repeat issues → training or tighter rules; report on violations by department.
