Interview Edge by Arahasoft

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026. This policy covers the Interview Edge app for Android and the Interview Edge website.

The short version

Interview Edge does not have accounts and does not collect your personal information. Your practice progress, your answers and the AI interviewer all stay on your device.

Microphone and voice

  • The app asks for microphone permission only so you can answer questions out loud. You can say no and type instead.
  • In the Android app, your speech is turned into text by your phone’s built-in speech recognition (provided by your phone’s maker or Google, under their own privacy terms). Interview Edge does not record, store or send your audio.
  • Spoken questions and replies use your phone’s built-in text-to-speech.

The AI interviewer

  • In the Android app the AI model (Google Gemma) is downloaded once and then runs entirely on your phone. Your answers and the AI’s replies are not sent to Arahasoft or anyone else.
  • The model file is downloaded from Hugging Face, which sees an ordinary download request (like any website visit).
  • If you choose “Interview me in Claude”, the app opens Claude (by Anthropic) in your browser; what you type there is covered by Anthropic’s privacy policy.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (questions marked known or to review, starred questions, practice history, settings and any profile details you enter) is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app or using “Reset all progress” in Settings deletes it.

Reporting AI replies

Each AI reply has a Report link. Reporting hides the reply and opens your email app with the reply text addressed to support@arahasoft.com; nothing is sent unless you send that email.

Purchases

If you buy the one-time unlock, payment is handled by Google Play. Arahasoft receives no card details.

Analytics, ads and sharing

The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not sell or share personal data.

Children

Interview Edge is intended for adults preparing for job interviews and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update the date above. Questions: support@arahasoft.com · Arahasoft, East Windsor, New Jersey, USA.
